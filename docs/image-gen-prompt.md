# Image-Gen Prompts

Concept image only; not fabrication authority. Do not include cut-ready dimensions, hole locations, or toolpaths.

## Hero Prompt

A finished box-style wooden tongue drum photographed as a real physical object on a clean neutral studio background: a rectangular hollow hardwood box, roughly shoebox-proportioned and longer than it is wide, sitting upright on a tabletop. The top face is the signature surface — six tongues cut into the wood in a paired-slit pattern, where slender narrow slits leave flat cantilever tongues of varying lengths that all lie flush with the top plane, the longer tongues reading visually as the lower bass notes and the shorter ones as higher notes. The grain runs lengthwise along the tongues; the wood is a warm richly figured hardwood such as padauk or walnut with a hand-rubbed satin oil finish, joined box sides with clean mitered or rabbeted corners. A pair of soft round-headed wooden mallets rests beside or across the drum for scale and storytelling, implying it is meant to be struck by hand. Realistic scale cues from the tabletop and mallets, soft directional studio light raking gently across the slit pattern to show depth, 3/4 product-photography angle, ~50mm lens, high detail, photorealistic, no text labels.

## In-Context Prompt

The same six-tongue wooden tongue drum staged in a woodworker's shop, resting on a workbench amid faint shavings and a couple of hand tools, with a pair of mallets poised just above the tongues as if mid-play. Warm natural window light rakes across the top surface, revealing the depth of the cut slits and the figure of the hardwood. Shallow depth of field keeps the drum and the playing hands sharp while the bench and shop fall into soft bokeh, environmental storytelling of a maker's acoustics study, concept visualization only.

## Detail Prompt

Macro close-up of the tongue field on the drum's top face: two or three adjacent cantilever tongues defined by clean narrow slits cut through the hardwood, each tongue a flat strip clamped at the slit terminus and free at the tip, the cut edges crisp and the surrounding surface flush. Raking low-angle light grazes across the slits to throw fine shadow lines and emphasize the saw-kerf walls, the warm grain, and the satin finish. High detail, tactile wood texture, no measurements.

## Negative / Avoid

No text, logos, watermarks, fake maker marks, brand names, or magazine mastheads; no dimension callouts or measurement lines; no impossible mechanisms or floating tongues; tongues must lie flush and stay attached at the slit ends; anatomically plausible hands if a player is shown.
