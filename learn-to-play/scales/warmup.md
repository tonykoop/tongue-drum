# Tongue Drum Warm-Up: G Pentatonic

## Scale walk
```
X:1
T:G Major Pentatonic Walk
M:4/4
L:1/4
Q:1/4=72
K:G
G A B d | e d B A | G B d e | d B A G |
```
*(No F or C — just G A B D E. All combinations sound good!)*

## Free improvisation guide
The G pentatonic scale has no "wrong" combinations. Try:
- Playing any two adjacent tongues together
- Walking from the lowest G up to the highest G (octave)
- Alternating between low and high versions of the same note (G2 ↔ G3 ↔ G4)

**Tip:** After any melody, return to the root G. The G is your "home base" — it always resolves.
