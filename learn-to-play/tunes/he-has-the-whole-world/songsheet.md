**Range:** D3–E4  **Source:** Traditional spiritual, public domain

## Notes
- Bouncy, upbeat feel — stronger quarter notes, steady pace.
- All 5 pentatonic notes (G A B D E) are used here.
- The "bridge" (bars 9–12) moves through more of the scale — it's the most challenging part.
- The final G4 resolves confidently back to the root.

**Provenance:** Traditional gospel/spiritual. Popularized in recordings from the 1920s–1950s,
but the melody origin is earlier and firmly public domain.
