# Notes: He's Got the Whole World in His Hands

**Provenance:** Traditional spiritual melody. Multiple PD recordings pre-1929.
The melody itself predates all documented recordings and is public domain.

**Range check:** D3–E4. The D (d in ABC) is the 5th degree of G — a strong supporting note.

**Practice tips:**
1. The repeated G (three G's then A) in bars 1, 5, 9 is the hook — make it groove.
2. The E (e in bars 6, 11) is the highest note — prepare for it by finding it first in the warmup.
3. At ♩= 100, this feels marching and confident — don't let the tempo drag.
