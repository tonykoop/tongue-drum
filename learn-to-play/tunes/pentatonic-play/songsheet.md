# Pentatonic Play — Songsheet

**Tempo:** Free (60–80 BPM)  **Meter:** Free  **Difficulty:** ★☆☆

## Quick reference
- 5 notes: G A B D E
- No sharps, no flats needed
- Every combination of these notes is harmonious
- Always resolve back to G

## Suggested 8-bar improvisation structure
| Bar | Idea |
|-----|------|
| 1–2 | Start on G, walk up to E |
| 3–4 | Play from E back down to G |
| 5–6 | Emphasize the D (the 5th) — pairs with G |
| 7–8 | Return to G, end on a long sustained G |
