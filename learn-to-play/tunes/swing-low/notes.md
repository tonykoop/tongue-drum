# Notes: Swing Low, Sweet Chariot

**Provenance:** Traditional African-American spiritual, documented in the 1840s.
Popularized by the Fisk Jubilee Singers (1870s). Fully public domain.
This is the lead-sheet melody only — no specific arrangement rights.

**Range check:** G3–E4. Uses G A B D E across one octave.

**Practice tips:**
1. The swing in the title is rhythmic — play the eighth-note pairs with a slight triplet feel.
2. The descending B–A–G figure (bars 6, 14) is the emotional "sighing" gesture of the spiritual.
3. Sing along while playing to internalize the melody before focusing on mallet technique.
