## Notes
- The pickup D (before bar 1) is the "swing" — enter confidently.
- The melody jumps around the pentatonic scale — G B D E are the core notes.
- The descending pattern B A G in bars 6 and 14 is a signature "swoop" gesture.
- Final G (4 beats) should ring fully — a satisfying conclusion.

**Provenance:** Traditional African-American spiritual, c. 1840s. Fisk Jubilee Singers recording
(1909) is historical but PD. The melody itself is firmly public domain.
