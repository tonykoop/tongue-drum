# Notes: Amazing Grace

**Provenance:** Lyrics by John Newton, "Olney Hymns" (1779). Tune "New Britain" is traditional
American folk melody, origin unknown, PD. Freshly arranged for G pentatonic tongue drum.

**Range check:** D3–E4. Uses all 5 pentatonic notes across two octaves.

**Practice tips:**
1. Amazing Grace is one of the most recognizable tunes — this is an excellent first complete song.
2. The dotted rhythm is the signature. Don't flatten it to even quarters.
3. In 3/4, feel a gentle sway rather than a march beat.
4. The chord progression underneath is I–V–I–IV–I — your G pentatonic naturally implies all of these.
