# Amazing Grace — Songsheet (Tongue Drum)

**Key:** G pentatonic  **Tempo:** ♩= 76  **Meter:** 3/4  **Difficulty:** ★★☆
**Range:** D3–E4  **Source:** John Newton (1779) / trad. tune "New Britain", PD

## Music
```
X:2 T:Amazing Grace M:3/4 L:1/4 Q:1/4=76 K:G
D2 | G3/2 B/ G E | D2 D | D3/2 G/ B2 | G3 |
B3/2 d/ B G | A2 B | G3/2 E/ G D | G3 |
(repeat verse)
```

## Pentatonic note check
Notes used: G A B D E — all five of your pentatonic tongues! No accidentals needed.

## Notes
- The dotted quarter + eighth rhythm (G3/2 B/) is "GRACE" — long-short.
- The 3/4 waltz feel: count "1-2-3" slowly. The pickup D (bar 1) lands just before "1".
- The high E (e in bar 13) is the melodic peak — give it space.

**Provenance:** Lyrics: John Newton, 1779, PD. Tune "New Britain": traditional American, PD.
