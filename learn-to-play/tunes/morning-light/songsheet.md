# Morning Light — Songsheet

**Key:** G major pentatonic  **Tempo:** ♩= 88  **Meter:** 4/4  **Difficulty:** ★★★  
**Range:** D3–E4  **License:** MIT (original — Heifer Zephyr)

## Character
A fresh, hopeful piece that rises from the root G through the full pentatonic scale and
returns. Each phrase ends with a sustained whole note (8 eighth-notes = 4 beats).
The arc: G rises to E (the 6th, the "morning" note), then descends back to G.

## Notes
- The E4 (e, bar 2) is the melodic peak — it's the "light" of Morning Light.
- The D3 (D, bar 10) is the lowest note — the "grounding" before the final ascent.
- Let each whole note ring completely before the next phrase. 4 beats = a full breath.
- This tune is a satisfying capstone after learning the 4 other tunes in this songbook.
