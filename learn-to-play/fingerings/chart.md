# Tongue Drum Layout Reference (G Major Pentatonic)

## 10T Medium layout (bilateral / two-bank)
```
Left bank (low)      Right bank (high)
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  G2    A2    B2    D3    E3             │
│  ─────────────────────────────          │
│  G3    A3    B3    D4    E4             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘
  ↑ play with left hand    ↑ right hand
  (bass/low)               (melody/high)
```

## Pentatonic scale reminders
- 5 notes: G A B D E
- No C, no F, no F#
- Every combination of G A B D E sounds good
- "Home base" is always G

## Playing technique
- Alternating hands (L-R-L-R) for fast passages
- Same-hand double strikes for emphasis
- Gentle wrist flick — don't press down after striking
- Soft mallets for meditation; hard mallets for rhythm playing
