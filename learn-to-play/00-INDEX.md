# Tongue Drum — Starter Songbook

Your tongue drum is tuned to **G major pentatonic**:

```
G A B D E
```

The pentatonic scale was chosen because every combination of these 5 notes sounds harmonious —
no "wrong" notes. This makes it ideal for improvisation and beginners.

## Your drum's range (by configuration)
| Config | Tongues | Notes |
|--------|---------|-------|
| 6T (Small) | 6 | G2 A2 B2 D3 E3 G3 |
| 8T (Small+) | 8 | G2 A2 B2 D3 E3 G3 A3 B3 |
| 10T (Medium) | 10 | G2 A2 B2 D3 E3 G3 A3 B3 D4 E4 |
| 12T (Medium+) | 12 | G2 A2 B2 D3 E3 G3 A3 B3 D4 E4 G4 A4 |
| 16T (Large) | 16 | G2 A2 B2 D3 E3 G3 A3 B3 D4 E4 G4 A4 B4 D5 E5 G5 |

This songbook is written for the **Medium 10T** configuration (G2–E4).
Transpose or adjust as needed for your specific build.

## Difficulty key
- ★☆☆ — Beginner: adjacent tongues, slow patterns
- ★★☆ — Intermediate: wider leaps, simple melodies
- ★★★ — Advanced: full range, rhythmic patterns

## Mallet notes
- Soft rubber or silicone mallets for warmest sustained tone.
- Hard rubber mallets for brighter, shorter attacks.
- Strike each tongue near its center, not at the tip or base of the slit.
